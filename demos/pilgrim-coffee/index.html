⟢ Our Ethos · आमची गोष्ट 01

From the hills of India, to your hands.

We are coffee pilgrims. As a multi-roaster house, we travel for the bean — to the best roasters and misty estates across India — and we bring it home to a warm little room in Nashik.

Here, nothing is rushed. The beans rotate, the water is dialled in, the grind is weighed. The result is a coffee with somewhere to be from — bright, honest, and deeply its own.
