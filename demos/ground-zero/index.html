Manifesto / 01

We started from the ground up.

Ground Zero is where it begins — the first micro-roastery in a city that had never roasted its own.

We buy green coffee straight from Indian estates that do it right, pay farmers fairly, and roast it ourselves in batches small enough to taste every one. No middlemen, no warehouse staleness, no mystery blends. Just honest coffee with a roast date you can trust.
